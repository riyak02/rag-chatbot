# Acme Gadgets — Customer Policy Handbook

## Warranty Policy

All Acme Gadgets products come with a standard 24-month limited warranty
from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in
materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.

The warranty does NOT cover:
- Accidental damage, including drops, spills, or water exposure
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear such as battery degradation after 18 months
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality

Customers can extend their warranty by an additional 12 months by
purchasing the Acme Care+ plan within 30 days of the original purchase.

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided
the product is returned in its original packaging with all accessories.
After 30 days but within 90 days, customers are eligible for store credit
only, not a cash refund.

Refunds are not available once the standard warranty period (24 months)
has expired. If a product fails after the warranty period, customers may
still use the paid repair service described below.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable under all
circumstances.

## Repair Service

Acme operates a paid, out-of-warranty repair service for products older
than 24 months. Repair costs are quoted after diagnostic inspection and
typically range from $40 to $150 depending on the issue. Turnaround time
is 5-10 business days.

## Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the continental US.
Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional
$15 fee. International shipping times vary by destination and customs
processing.

## Contact & Support

Support is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST, via email at
support@acmegadgets.example or live chat on the Acme website. Average
first-response time is under 4 hours during business hours.
